# Native prefix accounting and worker-result verification

Use for fresh-substrate restarts with partial preparation, asynchronous executor builds, and recipe-specific terminal results. Complements `partial-preparation-handoff-corrections.md` and `protected-driver-restoration-and-qualification-boundaries.md`.

## Count executed exposure separately from successful forming

Contact-finding forming attempts remain completed stress exposures. Three contact-finding sweeps followed by one successful positive half mean four executed sweeps, one successful forming event, and four native energy contributions. With health after every tenth stress branch, health is due after the sixth additional branch. Do not replay unsuccessful attempts or reset exposure accounting to the number of successes. Obtain operator confirmation that retained native records belong to the fresh device before rebinding them; pose similarity alone is insufficient.

A partial first-device handoff resumes only its missing suffix: negative forming plus each pending conditioning pair, not approach or completed positive forming. Preparation exposure and candidate-cycle counters stay separate.

## Confirm policy scope without manufacturing authorization

A prior campaign-wide cumulative-energy waiver is not automatically a new-substrate waiver. Where scope is ambiguous, ask once whether to extend the same policy to the named new batch. Freeze that explicit answer in a new execution overlay and bind exact device/candidate/recipe hashes. Distinguish conditioning/write branch warnings, cumulative sampled-energy stops, forming/read ceilings, cycle caps, and UNKNOWN/control/cleanup gates. Changing one does not waive the others; measured energy continues accumulating.

Validate clarification answers semantically. An asynchronous completion notice or timeout transcript appearing in an answer field is not a numeric count or operator confirmation. Preserve genuine answers to independent questions and re-ask only the unanswered item.

## Recover worker artifacts, not worker claims

A coding-agent timeout neither proves that nothing was written nor that an executor exists. Inspect the retained files, run the actual tests, and exercise compilation/default no-execution behavior. Missing scheduler/CLI tests must remain failures until implemented; do not label pure helper functions a runnable executor. After recovery, test the real backend-to-driver receipt shape, raw-before-parse persistence, exact command/run/parameter identity, prefix-only recovery, no replay of RECEIVED/UNKNOWN work, and refusal of cycle 301.

Use narrowly bounded follow-up coding tasks rather than repeating an oversized delegation. Children adapting software must have explicit no-hardware scope. Require their returned paths and test evidence, then verify independently before launch.

## Preserve the actual acquisition unit

Continuous ten-cycle batches must remain continuous native operations. A generic one-process-per-cycle rule does not authorize splitting them into branch calls that toggle output OFF. Qualify complete full-precision arrays with output OFF and use supported measurement-range ceilings while preserving requested compliance.

An optional guarded-subsystem physical-qualification flag is not a blanket verdict on a distinct reference-operational acquisition path. Inspect that path's real contract. Never promote output-OFF list proof to physical timing/energy-cutoff qualification, but do not invent an unavailable-path blocker solely from unrelated optional metadata.

## Verify terminal results before reporting or moving

Process exit zero is not a scientific success criterion. Read the durable journal, require every latest operation COMPLETE, validate raw receipt hashes and all native rows, recompute cycle metrics, and independently verify safe cleanup. Preserve the distinction between candidate cycles, preparation branches, and health operations.

A stop at the configured minimum cycle boundary because repeated within-sweep branch contrast fails is a recipe-specific terminal result, not demonstrated irreversible device damage or measured lifetime. Report weak retained-state evidence without turning sub-nA magnitude alone into a definitive contact diagnosis.

Export PNG plus full ordered CSV, cycle metrics and a matched result JSON containing batch/candidate/device/recipe identity, native receipt hashes, terminal reason and cleanup evidence. Keep preparation separate from candidate output. Advance an authorized screen only after the terminal/no-UNKNOWN/safe-travel gates; report the next device's actual verified state rather than claiming its measurements have started.
