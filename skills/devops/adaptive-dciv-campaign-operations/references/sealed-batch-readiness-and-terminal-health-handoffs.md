# Sealed batch readiness and terminal-health handoffs

## Once-per-device preparation
User correction: forming and preparation/conditioning are device checkpoints, never prerequisites to repeat before each candidate batch. Preserve their full completed prefix. Safety/readiness checks and scheduled health reads are separate obligations. A continuation transports only the next unperformed operations and counts every prior completed cycle toward the total cap.

## Long batches versus short readiness validity
A native batch can outlast a valid readiness context. Do not extend historical timestamps, relax expiry guards, or infer uncertain exposure from process exit alone. Verify the journal plus raw/native start-command-complete receipts. Distinguish expiry before the owed health INTENT from uncertainty during acquisition.

A tested conservative pattern is a uniquely reserved bounded continuation: genuinely fresh readiness, owed health first, at most one next candidate batch, independent OFF/open cleanup, explicit HEALTH_OWED stop. Obtain new readiness after the batch before its scheduled health. Preserve original UNKNOWN journals and reservations byte-for-byte. Inherit exact cycle progress, failure latch/streak, recipe binding, forming/preparation checkpoint and cumulative energy once. Never call a cap reached with health owed a successful device completion.

## Cleanup failure versus stress uncertainty
An edge restart is not matrix-open recovery. Obtain native SMU-OFF, open-all success and matrix-nil readback, empty errors and unchanged pose. Audit the failed cleanup lifecycle separately from completed acquisition. Scope any reconciliation to the exact failed control operations; do not relabel old failures as successful or admit arbitrary UNKNOWN histories. Preserve returned raw evidence and check all chain identities/hashes before allowing another device.

A returned near-compliance final health sample remains a terminal safety stop even when all requested cycles completed. Retain any earlier recipe-failure latch. Verify final OFF/open cleanup independently. Reconcile solely for terminal bookkeeping and safe next-device progression, never to authorize additional stress on that device.

## Provenance and delivery
Chained continuation metadata can select the wrong historical reservation in a generic next-device resolver. Resolve the exact sealed compiled identity and corresponding claim/context; do not guess directories or rewrite sealed hashes. A narrow versioned adapter may preserve shared pinned modules while validating the entire chain. Use the exact interpreter that reproduces sealed compiler hashes.

Subagent timeout does not prove artifacts absent or ready. Read its artifacts, rerun tests and pinned compilation, and inspect reservation/output state before any launch. A running process is not an acquisition receipt: report the actual operation/run identity and journal state.

## Frozen cycle plots
For multi-journal candidate snapshots, select only COMPLETE candidate acquisitions, validate raw hashes and native segments, deduplicate run identities, and assert a contiguous exact cycle range with both polarity segments. Export CSV with source/run/segment provenance. Plot commanded voltage versus log absolute current using PF solid, PB dashed, NF dotted, NB dash-dot; label pending health and failure/stop state. Never include forming or preparation in a candidate-cycle count. A snapshot manifest must distinguish verified candidate exposure from an UNKNOWN enclosing journal.
