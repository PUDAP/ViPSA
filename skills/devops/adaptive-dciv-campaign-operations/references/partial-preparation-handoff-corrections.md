# Partial-preparation and multi-bot handoff corrections

Companion to `fresh-substrate-training-cutoff-and-prime-grid-handoff.md`.

## Operator confirmation versus stale package text

When an operator explicitly chooses a protocol in a clarification form, retain the actual response and scope as authorization. A separate bot may miss that response and publish immutable files with null protocol fields or a statement that settings are unresolved. Do not ask the operator the identical policy question again solely because the bot missed it. Preserve the proposal files and hashes; make a separate execution overlay containing the confirmed protocol, source of confirmation and any later corrections. New software/deployment choices that materially change the protocol remain separate decisions, not implied authorization.

## First-device preparation is a prefix, not a Boolean

Manual contact, positive forming, negative forming, conditioning and candidate testing are separate states. An instruction not to replay preparation does not mean all preparation has completed. If the operator corrects the handoff to 'positive forming complete; negative half and six conditioning cycles pending':

1. Supersede the earlier blanket skip-preparation instruction in the operator request, handoff, watcher prompt and execution plan.
2. Record the positive half as operator-reported complete; do not replay it. Record the negative half and each of the six conditioning cycles as pending.
3. Bind native receipts when available, but do not silently attribute nearby historical sweeps to the newly loaded substrate just because pose or timestamps look similar.
4. Preserve current contact. Do not insert automatic approach, alignment or retraction merely to fit an old launcher.
5. Resume only the verified missing suffix under the confirmed protocol; keep preparation counters separate from candidate endurance and reconcile health cadence rather than assuming the old substrate's counter carries over.

## Recipe controls are not a native protocol

Unsigned RESET magnitudes, dwell and compliance parameters do not specify signed arrays, branch order, read policy or output continuity. Before any exposure, compare the frozen compiler and controller requirements with the currently loaded edge's actual capabilities. Separate sweep calls that turn output OFF between branches are not automatically equivalent to continuous native ten-cycle batches. A mismatch is grounds to hold execution and obtain a deployment decision; it is not proof that restoring or porting a driver has been qualified. Preserve the tested current driver and recipe package while deciding. Never present an unimplemented replacement as a working recovery.

## Grid labels versus traversal

Two maps may both be serpentine yet assign different names: row-major physical numbering visits `1'..10',20'..11'`, while traversal-sequential numbering visits `1'..10',11'..20'` with the second row reversed in X. Keep device ID, row/column, traversal index and native target separate. Compare all coordinates programmatically, preserve the operator-approved map and resolve disagreement before the first divergent target. Agreement for the first eight assignments does not validate the other 92 positions.

## Watcher ownership

When an interactive executor takes over a received handoff, pause the handoff-triggered watcher or acquire the shared owner before publishing a launchable handoff. A parent-review flag alone is not a process lock. On a real blocker, persist its status and whether any hardware was dispatched; do not leave an unattended watcher free to launch a conflicting controller.
