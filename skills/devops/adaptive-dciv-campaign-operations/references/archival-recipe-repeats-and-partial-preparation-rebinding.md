# Archival recipe repeats with retained physical preparation

Use when the operator changes from newly proposed recipes to an unchanged historical batch, especially after preparing the current physical device.

## Separate source identity, repeat identity, and physical history

1. Freeze the requested historical batch and original recipe order. Verify every original recipe against its source manifest hash, and preserve byte-identical archival copies plus full-precision parameters. Similar optimized values are not equivalent to the requested historical recipe.
2. Create an explicit original-recipe-index → fresh-device binding manifest, using the approved fresh grid origin and traversal. Old absolute coordinates inside archival files are provenance only, never fresh motion targets. Verify the requested assignment count and exclusions programmatically.
3. Preserve original candidate IDs and hashes as provenance. A deliberately requested new physical repeat needs a distinct repeat-request/execution identity; do not mutate the original records or impersonate a prior completed candidate.
4. Rebind physical preparation separately. Forming traces, executed motion, low-bias diagnostics, sampled energy, and health cadence belong to the physical device even when its candidate recipe changes. Reference immutable original native receipts and hashes in a new evidence artifact carrying the new recipe binding. Record why these are preparation exposures rather than candidate cycles. Never replay approach, positive forming, or a completed preparation prefix just to obtain a new candidate identity.
5. Count all completed forming-stress attempts for preparation health cadence, including contact-finding attempts. Distinguish the count of successful forming events from total exposure count. Keep preparation and candidate endurance counters separate.
6. Use an isolated executor/package/journal namespace for a recipe-family change. Verify exact device identifiers, fresh verified pose, candidate/request IDs, full arrays, owner lock, energy-policy scope, and no-replay semantics. A new destination outside the prior device range must not silently inherit a device-scoped motion ceiling or waiver.

## Handoff versus execution

A file or @mention is not another bot's acknowledgment, and a prepared handoff is not a running measurement. Record the executing actor and actual process/operation evidence. After the user approves continuation, use one controller and prevent duplicate launches. Status replies must say whether hardware is actually measuring; if idle, name the last verified operation and the next unstarted phase rather than describing queued work as active.

## Pre-dispatch recovery without mechanical replay

A software rejection before backend identity reservation, connection, or publish can establish zero electrical exposure only when the exact source ordering and absence of outgoing wire artifacts corroborate it. Preserve the failed attempt and original UNKNOWN history. Use a new recovery journal/request identity, revalidate safety, and resume at the missing operation.

Successful earlier motion remains successful: a completed pre-approach must not be repeated after an OFF-proof command is rejected locally. Freeze and verify the current pose instead. An empty trace alone never establishes zero exposure after dispatch.

Exercise the entire launcher path offline—not only pure waveform helpers—including output-directory guards, backend command allowlists, context selection, and recovery skip logic. Keep journals and raw receipts inside the executor's allowed write root. Use the already-authorized OFF-only validation path for native list proofs rather than widening a stress backend's scope merely to admit another command.

## Native and scientific semantics

- Preserve the authorized native acquisition unit, such as ten uninterrupted full cycles. Process-isolation advice must not split it or insert output toggles.
- For forming-as-approach, a conservative motion stop examines the full ordered PF/PB trace. Three consecutive near-limit return-branch points already prohibit another approach increment or positive stress attempt. Distinguish this safety stop from any separately defined outbound forming-qualification criterion; it does not prove retained switching.
- Read-compliance hits require commanded versus measured voltage and resistance-bound wording, not a clean ohmic value or an automatic claim of irreversible damage.
- Report recipe-specific contrast failure, irreversible physical failure, and right-censoring at the configured cap as different outcomes. Use the campaign's actual cap, not a historical constant copied from a reference.

## Verified worked pattern

A historical eight-recipe batch was hash-verified and mapped in order onto fresh devices 2'–9', excluding spent 1'. Physical device 2' had already completed two positive forming attempts, with the motion stop reached at Z +55. Its approach, native forming receipts, and sampled energy were preserved and rebound to historical recipe 1 without replay. The isolated repeat executor passed mapping/control checks; its SET/RESET arrays and five unique preparation arrays were qualified with output OFF. The pending negative preparation half then completed with native validation and verified cleanup, and a tracked worker continued conditioning. This verifies the packaging, rebinding, OFF-proof and resumed-preparation path—not completion of the entire repeated batch.
