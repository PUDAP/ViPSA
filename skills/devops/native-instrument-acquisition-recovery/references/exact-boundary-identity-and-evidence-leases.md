# Exact-boundary continuation: tested recovery lessons

## Preserve exposure, not stale phase labels

An ENOSPC-interrupted worker retained a misleading preparation-not-started status while native lifecycles proved four forming sweeps and seven conditioning operations complete. Audit journal intents against exact native request/reply identities before selecting the first missing operation. Preserve originals, append reconciliation, and never replay completed stress. A continuation's cumulative energy can already include the original ledger; calculate incremental energy separately rather than summing cumulative totals.

## Mandatory health and terminal outcomes

A context-expired worker completed ten cycles and a failure latch before its mandatory health read was dispatched. An isolated, once-claimed continuation performed only the missing health read under fresh ownership and native safety checks. The health returned near compliance; cleanup was verified and further stress was prohibited. Preserve the known candidate failure separately from original and continuation UNKNOWN flags. Do not skip an undispatched mandatory health operation or repeat a returned safety stop.

## Terminal-context integrity

A negative regression exposed a real omission: terminal reconciliation did not invoke the complete context validator. Validate context, its schema/readiness proof, compiled recipe, historical boundary and source journal; bind the context digest to the permanent one-shot claim. Test authority-field changes, proof-hash drift, and self-consistent replacement of both context and proof. Mere internal consistency is insufficient.

## Literal identifiers and device rebinding

Global replacement of '58' with '59' also altered the timestamp substring in a campaign identifier. Native motion completed under the altered key while the compiler retained the original logical frame. Never globally replace digits in source. Rebind exact parameters or AST literals and test unchanged identifiers and waveform values. For an existing completed operation, retain the literal stored token, explicitly audit drift, validate the entire native suffix and permit only a narrow completed-operation alias. Never rewrite the database key or repeat navigation under the expected key.

## Mutable-journal versus immutable-file evidence

A real Linux lease regression reproduced the controller invalidating its own evidence lease when its legitimate journal append touched a file pinned as immutable. Partition only the exact live canonical journal by role; validate its exact baseline and allowed durable suffix under exclusive locks. Keep other evidence hash-pinned and kernel-monitored. Tests must reject arbitrary excluded paths, historical-row edits, unjournaled native evidence and replay after uncertain motion. Offline full integration passed for this partition and exact stopped-prefix continuation; the final live continuation was launched but had no returned result at the end of this review. Do not present that last live path as completed.

## Release and orchestration checks

Run slow historical validation before fresh owner attestation; retain original TTL and native freshness guards. Refresh only at explicitly tested stable boundaries. Preserve current-source hashes and negative tests; an earlier pass does not validate subsequent edits. Worker timeout, background-handle tracking loss and test completion are distinct: inspect existing processes and durable reports before restarting tests. Report launched worker, completed navigation, image approval and actual electrical acquisition separately.
