# Known health stops and ML outcomes

## Continuous-campaign preference

The user explicitly corrected stopping merely because a generic UNKNOWN executor flag exists. Under standing campaign authorization, reconcile exact native evidence and verified cleanup, then advance to the next saved assignment without another handoff. Do not bypass safety limits, replay stress or proceed through genuinely ambiguous acquisition/motion.

## Separate audit flags from scientific labels

A native health command can return successful measured rows yet fail the near-compliance validator. Preserve its original UNKNOWN journal event, raw receipt and measured exposure. Add an exact terminal assessment proving exposure accounted and cleanup verified; forbid further stress or health retries on that device.

Do not infer candidate recipe failure from health compliance. Some exercised devices had a candidate-failure latch; another had completed cycles with failure_streak=0 and no latch. These are distinct outcomes. Neither implies successful endurance.

For downstream datasets, separate executor status, reconciled outcome, completed cycles, nullable candidate-failure latch, health-stop reason, exposure accounting, and cleanup verification. Do not drop completed measurements or map UNKNOWN alone to an unknown scientific outcome. If reporting a one-sample terminal-health energy estimate outside the saved ledger, label it an estimate, not zero and not a physical bound.

## Validated recovery patterns

- Pre-dispatch context expiry with all prior events COMPLETE: audit the contiguous prefix, refresh owned native readiness, and perform only the exact missing operation. A latched failure forbids additional candidate cycles; a pending mandatory health check may remain due.
- Returned near-compliance health: no repeat; terminal evidence reconciliation only.
- Returned navigation moves with incomplete bookkeeping: audit matched native receipts and exact final pose; append only missing readback/cleanup evidence, never rerun travel. Fresh schema discovery is required before native adapter capture.
- Source-pinned additive adapters: finish code and mutation tests, freeze all executable files and inputs before writing canonical checkpoints. Later code changes invalidate evidence pins.

Report a launched worker as a launched worker, not proof of acquisition. Native measurement evidence is required before claiming measurement start.
