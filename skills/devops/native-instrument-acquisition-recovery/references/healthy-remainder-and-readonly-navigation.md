# Exercised sequential recovery patterns

## Healthy remainder versus failure-latched health

An expiry-only stop after ten completed cycles was recovered with one exact pending health read. Healthy health with no failure latch was nonterminal, not permission to skip the device. A tested additive controller then executed only batch02 and its due health; it stopped at returned near-compliance health. Twenty cycles were preserved, and failure latched at cycle13 remained meaningful despite a final failure streak of zero. A separate failure-latched expiry recovery completed only its pending batch02 health and terminated as known recipe-specific failure.

Before continuation, audit ordered compiled operations, native lifecycle receipts and hashes, actual parameters, complete historical metrics, claims, final pose, and inherited energy. Preserve original UNKNOWN journals. Carry the cumulative ledger forward once; never sum multiple cumulative ledgers. Keep returned safety-rejected health exposure separately accounted, never silently zero it. No retry after near-compliance health.

For long runs, fresh readiness is needed before each safe operation boundary: owner lock, actual OFF/open/errors/pose/deployment receipts, source/sole-edge checks, and TTL rechecked at dispatch. Timestamp-only renewal is not fresh acquisition evidence. Preserve original TTL. Distinguish inherited illumination evidence from a newly observed light state.

## Returned navigation with missing checkpoints

Read-only recovery was exercised both when all moves returned but final cleanup readback was missing, and when the Y response returned but post-Y capture itself expired. Do not fabricate historical post-Y. Preserve STOP/error history and independently capture fresh target pose, idle/null-run state, confirmed mechanical state, OFF/open/errors and source provenance under existing locks. A capability-restricted recovery issues getters only—no motion or cleanup replay. Only after exact proof may an additive final checkpoint and exact-operation completion be committed, then read back.

Retain strict earlier recovered-prefix validators in every later adapter. Pin the exact operation rows/native evidence rather than a mutable whole-database hash when the campaign must append later operations. This is not permission to drop historical validation. After finalization, review the real image for geometry only, ensure lights OFF, then acquire through the recovery-aware adapter.

## Orchestration lessons

A timed-out child can leave an expensive test process running. Use its eventual result rather than assuming failure or starting redundant suites. Freeze only finished tested artifacts. Background completion under standing auto-next authorization triggers the next verified campaign step, not only a status message. Report worker launch separately from actual native acquisition. These patterns were exercised; no unfinished later acquisition is certified here.
